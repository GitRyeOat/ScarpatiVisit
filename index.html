The Method

A quiet conversation between two beings

The Scarpati method is a unique approach to horsemanship that Oscar Scarpati learned and adapted from the native horsemen of Argentina. It preserves a kind of lost wisdom — knowledge passed down first to Oscar, and then to his son Cristobal and Cristobal's wife Flor.

To watch them quietly and calmly address an untrained horse is educational and inspiring. It is more like witnessing a dance than a training session — two beings learning to come together and understand one another. Often barefoot, wearing traditional cotton clothes and caps, they work body on body, letting the horse become accustomed to human touch.

Their work establishes a meaningful respect and a pathway to lifelong communication.

The Family

A rare treasure — a family that travels and trains together

Their skill and regard for horses is supreme and unquestionable. Through their efforts, the remarkable knowledge and skills of Argentina have spread across the globe. For the last 20 years they have trained others in Europe and across South, Central, and North America.

After a once-in-a-lifetime invitation to compete at Road to the Horse in Kentucky, Cristobal and Flor not only witnessed remarkable horsemen — they found a profound validation of their own methods, and a reinvigoration in the values and principles they carry around the world.

The family runs a school at their home in San Luis, Argentina. Their international clinics and demonstrations have been offered since 2014 — many of them right here at Blue Dot in Nicasio.

Why We're Gathering

Oscar's road back

Oscar, now in his 80s, spent most of his life on the same mission. He established the work that Cristobal now champions.

Earlier this year, Oscar was thrown from his mule and suffered a spinal cord injury, leaving him unable to move, walk, or work with horses. The road to healing has been extremely slow and difficult. Each day is a challenge.

He recently took his first few steps. Doctors are hopeful for recovery — but expect it to take 9 to 18 months of intense rehabilitation.

With Flor also expecting a baby, Cristobal has taken over as the sole practitioner and representative for the family. This evening is how our community shows up for them.
